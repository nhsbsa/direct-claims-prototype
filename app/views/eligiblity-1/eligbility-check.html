Check claim eligibility

Review the claim form and evidence to check whether the claim can be accepted.

When to reject the claim form

Reject the claim form immediately if:

  • the treatment was not in an EU country, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, or Switzerland
  • the treatment was on a cruise ship
  • the customer is claiming for treatment in their country of residence within the dates of their registered S1

If you've already contacted the customer for more information, you can reject the claim form if:

  • the customer is living abroad and does not have a valid S1
  • the customer is studying abroad and cannot provide evidence of study
  • the treatment was pre-planned
  • the treatment was not medically necessary
  • the customer has not provided original documents or a good reason for sending copies
  • the customer has not paid any of their treatment costs
  • the evidence shows that the UK is not responsible

When to request more information

Review the claim form and evidence to check whether the claim can be accepted.

Part 1 - Patient's details

Request more information if:

  • any necessary information is missing, such as name or date of birth
  • the customer is living abroad and does not appear to have a registered S1

You do not need to request the customer's National Insurance number or NHS number.

Part 2 - Nationality

Request more information if:

  • any necessary information is missing, such as nationality
  • nationality evidence is missing, if required
  • evidence of relationship is missing, if required
Part 3 - Travel details

Request more information if:

  • any necessary information is missing, such as travel dates
  • they're working abroad but have not provided an A1, or do not have an S1
  • they're studying abroad but have not provided evidence of study
Part 4 - Details of charges paid

Request more information if:

  • any necessary information is missing, such as amount paid
Part 5 - Treatment details

Request more information if:

  • any necessary information is missing, such as treatment dates
  • the treatment was in multiple countries - the customer will need to send separate claim forms for each country
  • multiple separate treatments are listed - the customer will need to send separate claim forms for each treatment
  • the treatment appears to be pre-planned
  • the treatment appears to not be medically necessary
Part 6 - Declaration

Request more information if:

  • the signature or date is missing
Evidence

Request more information if:

  • the customer has provided copied evidence
  • any invoices are unpaid
  • the evidence suggests the treatment was private, pre-planned, or not medically necessary/li>
