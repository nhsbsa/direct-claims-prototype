What country is this claim from?

Mandatory

Non-UK or Irish nationals must provide evidence of their right to reside in the UK. This might be a share code or Indefinite Leave to Remain documentation.

If the treatment was in Switzerland, and the customer has selected 'Norwegian, Icelandic, or Liechtenstein national' or 'Other nationality', they must be a family member of someone covered under the UK-Swiss convention. They must provide evidence of this relationship.
